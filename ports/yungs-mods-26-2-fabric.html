YUNG's mods for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric)

12 of YUNG's mods have no official Minecraft 26.2 Fabric build yet (their newest Fabric builds are for 26.1.2). Community ports of all of them run on 26.2: the Modkeel lab ran them in a modpack on a real 26.2 server and client, where the server generated the 20 structures they add.

How the lab checked

On September 29, 2026, the lab put all 12 ports in its Keel: Better Structures modpack, 32 mods on Fabric 26.2, and started a real dedicated server: it was ready in 10 seconds. The test then generated every structure the pack's mods add, one by one with the server's own /place structure command: 197 in all (1 more cannot fit at the test spots, a known limit of those structures, not of the ports). A real client then started with the same mods and joined a world. Nothing crashed.

What they need

  • Cloth Config API, needed by every mod here but YUNG's API. Its 26.2 Fabric build is on its Modrinth page.
  • Fabric API, needed by every mod here. Its 26.2 Fabric build is on its Modrinth page.
  • YUNG's API, needed by every other mod here. It has no official 26.2 build either; its port is on this page.

No official 26.2 Fabric build yet; its author's newest Fabric build, 26.1.2-Fabric-6.1.1, is for Minecraft 26.1.2. Before you add them

  • Back up your world. A port is a stand-in: nobody can test yet whether the official builds will read everything a port wrote.
  • Switch to the official builds once their Modrinth pages list 26.2 for Fabric.

Trying these on 26.2?

Your pack is not the lab's: a port can fail in it. The Modkeel Companion, a free mod for Fabric, copies each world before it loads with different mods; if the game then crashes, it names the mod that most likely caused it and turns that mod off, or goes back to the mods that last worked, in one click.
