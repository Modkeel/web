What Modkeel gives you

Answers about modded Minecraft (which mod most likely caused a crash, where to get a mod for a Minecraft version, which mods ran together) drawn from the Modkeel lab's test runs, from reports players chose to share, and from public mod catalogs such as Modrinth. They are the best evidence we have, not a guarantee: a mod the lab never tested can still break a pack, and a suggested fix can fail on yours.

Back up before you change your mods

Changing mods can damage a world. Keep a backup before you add, remove or update mods, and before you install a port. The Companion backs up worlds when the mod set changes, but no backup tool replaces your own copy of a world you care about.

Mods and ports are their authors' work

Modkeel links to mods on their authors' pages and does not own them. Ports offered by Modkeel (the Modkeel/ports releases and community forks) are builds of other people's open-source mods for a version their author has not released yet. Each keeps its original licence, is not the author's own build, and should not be reported to the original author as their bug.
