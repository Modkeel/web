Lithium on Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3

On a plain Minecraft 26.3 server in the lab's benchmark, Lithium beat every run without it on 400 mobs (-52%), redstone (-37%) and new terrain (-5%); it lost to every one on memory after start (+6%).

Lithium releases started in real Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab. It ran in the same game as 8 other mods, among them FerriteCore, Entity Culling, ModernFix and Concurrent Chunk Management Engine (Fabric).

What the lab measured

Frame rate with Lithium, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1, Lithium, which speeds up game logic rather than drawing, did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 27 runs per number; changes within 54%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.
