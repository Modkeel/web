Sodium on Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3

Its biggest frame rate change in the Modkeel lab: on Minecraft 1.20.1, Sodium raised the frame rate 130% over terrain (210 to 482 FPS) and 27% with 300 mobs behind a wall (55 to 70 FPS); it lowered the frame rate 14% with 300 mobs in view (82 to 70 FPS), because on Windows with an NVIDIA card Sodium turns on a driver workaround against crashes that makes each draw call wait, and this version draws mobs of mixed kinds in many small draw calls.

Sodium releases started in real Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 26.1.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab. It ran in the same game as 7 other mods, among them Iris Shaders, Entity Culling, ImmediatelyFast and Mod Menu.

What the lab measured

Frame rate with Sodium, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3, Sodium raised the frame rate 55% with 300 mobs in view (94 to 145 FPS), 23% over terrain (234 to 288 FPS) and 78% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 256 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.2, Sodium raised the frame rate 61% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 179 FPS), 37% over terrain (283 to 388 FPS) and 88% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 168 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.1.2, Sodium raised the frame rate 51% with 300 mobs in view (124 to 188 FPS), 11% over terrain (347 to 386 FPS) and 75% with 300 mobs behind a wall (103 to 180 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.11, Sodium raised the frame rate 26% with 300 mobs in view (143 to 180 FPS), 52% over terrain (272 to 414 FPS) and 65% with 300 mobs behind a wall (105 to 174 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.1, Sodium raised the frame rate 86% over terrain (304 to 564 FPS); it lowered the frame rate 31% with 300 mobs in view (103 to 71 FPS), because on Windows with an NVIDIA card Sodium turns on a driver workaround against crashes that makes each draw call wait, and this version draws mobs of mixed kinds in many small draw calls.

On Minecraft 1.20.1, Sodium raised the frame rate 130% over terrain (210 to 482 FPS) and 27% with 300 mobs behind a wall (55 to 70 FPS); it lowered the frame rate 14% with 300 mobs in view (82 to 70 FPS), because on Windows with an NVIDIA card Sodium turns on a driver workaround against crashes that makes each draw call wait, and this version draws mobs of mixed kinds in many small draw calls.

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 27 runs per number; changes within 54%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.
