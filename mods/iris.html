What the lab measured

Frame rate with Iris Shaders, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 57% with 300 mobs in view (94 to 148 FPS), 14% over terrain (248 to 282 FPS) and 75% with 300 mobs behind a wall (148 to 258 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.2, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 61% with 300 mobs in view (110 to 178 FPS), 33% over terrain (286 to 380 FPS) and 97% with 300 mobs behind a wall (87 to 172 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.11, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 26% with 300 mobs in view (150 to 189 FPS), 45% over terrain (292 to 422 FPS) and 54% with 300 mobs behind a wall (117 to 180 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.1, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 64% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 182 FPS), 80% over terrain (324 to 583 FPS) and 90% with 300 mobs behind a wall (90 to 172 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.20.1, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 106% with 300 mobs in view (84 to 173 FPS), 117% over terrain (227 to 494 FPS) and 166% with 300 mobs behind a wall (58 to 156 FPS).

How it was measured: a real Fabric client on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H (an entry-level gaming laptop from 2020) joined the same world at 1280x720, render distance 24, VSync off. Each number is the median second of 20-second scenes over 2 to 8 runs. Runs without mods differed by up to 23%, so smaller changes are not counted. Frame rates depend on the computer: on a faster one the numbers here are usually higher. The percentage is the better guide, but it moves too: mods that cut rendering work (Sodium, More Culling) gain most when the graphics card is the limit, and mods that cut entities or game logic (Entity Culling, Lithium) when the processor is. Some effects belong to one GPU brand: Sodium's drop with mobs on 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 only happens on NVIDIA cards. Raw data: every run's frame rate per second, Minecraft and Fabric Loader versions, GPU and driver, loaded mods and the SHA-1 of each jar, which Modrinth matches to the exact release.
