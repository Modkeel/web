Iris Shaders on Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3

Its biggest frame rate change in the Modkeel lab: on Minecraft 1.20.1, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 105% with 300 mobs in view (82 to 167 FPS), 127% over terrain (210 to 475 FPS) and 178% with 300 mobs behind a wall (55 to 153 FPS).

Iris Shaders releases started in real Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab. It ran in the same game as 6 other mods, among them Sodium, ImmediatelyFast, Mod Menu and LambDynamicLights - Dynamic Lights.

Lab data as of October 15, 2026. Mod id iris.

What the lab measured

Frame rate with Iris Shaders, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 56% with 300 mobs in view (94 to 146 FPS), 13% over terrain (234 to 264 FPS) and 73% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 250 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.2, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 56% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 173 FPS), 32% over terrain (283 to 373 FPS) and 85% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 165 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.11, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 30% with 300 mobs in view (143 to 186 FPS), 46% over terrain (272 to 396 FPS) and 65% with 300 mobs behind a wall (105 to 173 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.1, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 76% with 300 mobs in view (103 to 181 FPS), 87% over terrain (304 to 569 FPS) and 103% with 300 mobs behind a wall (85 to 172 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.20.1, Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 105% with 300 mobs in view (82 to 167 FPS), 127% over terrain (210 to 475 FPS) and 178% with 300 mobs behind a wall (55 to 153 FPS).

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 27 runs per number; changes within 54%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.
