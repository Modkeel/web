Entity Culling on Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3

Its biggest frame rate change in the Modkeel lab: on Minecraft 26.1.2, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 143% with 300 mobs behind a wall (103 to 250 FPS).

Entity Culling releases started in real Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 26.1.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab. It ran in the same game as 9 other mods, among them Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore and ModernFix.

What the lab measured

Frame rate with Entity Culling, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 110% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 303 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.2, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 76% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 157 FPS).

On Minecraft 26.1.2, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 143% with 300 mobs behind a wall (103 to 250 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.11, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 97% with 300 mobs behind a wall (105 to 206 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.1, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 129% with 300 mobs behind a wall (85 to 194 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.20.1, Entity Culling raised the frame rate 102% with 300 mobs behind a wall (55 to 111 FPS).

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 27 runs per number; changes within 54%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.
