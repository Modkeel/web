ImmediatelyFast on Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3

Its biggest frame rate change in the Modkeel lab: on Minecraft 1.21.1, ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 40% with 300 mobs in view (103 to 144 FPS) and 28% with 300 mobs behind a wall (85 to 109 FPS).

ImmediatelyFast releases started in real Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab. It ran in the same game as 7 other mods, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Entity Culling and Mod Menu.

What the lab measured

Frame rate with ImmediatelyFast, against the same game without mods.

On Minecraft 26.3 and 26.2, ImmediatelyFast did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

On Minecraft 1.21.11, ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 6% with 300 mobs in view (143 to 152 FPS) and 14% with 300 mobs behind a wall (105 to 120 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.21.1, ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 40% with 300 mobs in view (103 to 144 FPS) and 28% with 300 mobs behind a wall (85 to 109 FPS).

On Minecraft 1.20.1, ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 19% with 300 mobs in view (82 to 97 FPS).

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 27 runs per number; changes within 54%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.
