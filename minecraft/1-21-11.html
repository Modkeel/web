Mods tested on Minecraft 1.21.11

Sodium mc1.21.11-0.8.15-beta.1-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.10.8+1.21.11-fabric loaded a world together on a client on Minecraft 1.21.11 in the Modkeel lab. 12 mod releases started in real Minecraft 1.21.11 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 31 pairs of mods in the same game.

Lab data as of October 4, 2026. 12 releases ran, 31 pairs.

What the lab measured on 1.21.11

Frame rate on Minecraft 1.21.11 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 33% with 300 mobs in view (150 to 199 FPS), 53% over terrain (292 to 446 FPS) and 59% with 300 mobs behind a wall (117 to 186 FPS).

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 26% with 300 mobs in view (150 to 189 FPS), 45% over terrain (292 to 422 FPS) and 54% with 300 mobs behind a wall (117 to 180 FPS).

Lithium, which speeds up game logic rather than drawing, lowered the frame rate 5% over terrain (292 to 276 FPS).

Entity Culling raised the frame rate 88% with 300 mobs behind a wall (117 to 220 FPS).

ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 14% over terrain (292 to 331 FPS).

More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

How it was measured: a real Fabric client on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H joined the same world at 1280x720, render distance 24, VSync off. Each number is the median second of 20-second scenes over 2 to 4 runs. Runs without mods differed by up to 12%, so smaller changes are not counted. Raw data: every run's frame rate per second, Minecraft and Fabric Loader versions, GPU and driver, loaded mods and the SHA-1 of each jar, which Modrinth matches to the exact release.

How much FPS does Sodium add on Minecraft 1.21.11?

In the Modkeel lab (data as of October 4, 2026), on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, Sodium raised the frame rate 33% with 300 mobs in view (150 to 199 FPS), 53% over terrain (292 to 446 FPS) and 59% with 300 mobs behind a wall (117 to 186 FPS) on Minecraft 1.21.11.

About this data

Loaded a world: a real Minecraft client started with the mod and joined a world. Server started: a real server finished starting with it. Benchmark: it ran through a timed lab scene. Did not start: the game stopped before it was playable.

Each row is one run in the Modkeel lab, matched to the exact file that ran: other files may behave differently. A mod or pair missing here was not tested yet; that is not a conflict.
