Mods tested on Minecraft 1.21.1

Sodium mc1.21.1-0.8.13-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.8.14-beta.1+1.21.1-fabric loaded a world together on a client on Minecraft 1.21.1 in the Modkeel lab. 14 mod releases started in real Minecraft 1.21.1 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 42 pairs of mods in the same game.

Lab data as of October 4, 2026. 14 releases ran, 42 pairs.

What the lab measured on 1.21.1

Frame rate on Minecraft 1.21.1 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 100% over terrain (324 to 650 FPS); it lowered the frame rate 33% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 74 FPS) and 20% with 300 mobs behind a wall (90 to 72 FPS), because on Windows with an NVIDIA card Sodium turns on a driver workaround against crashes that makes each draw call wait, and this version draws mobs of mixed kinds in many small draw calls.

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 64% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 182 FPS), 80% over terrain (324 to 583 FPS) and 90% with 300 mobs behind a wall (90 to 172 FPS).

Entity Culling raised the frame rate 140% with 300 mobs behind a wall (90 to 217 FPS).

ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 38% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 153 FPS) and 24% with 300 mobs behind a wall (90 to 112 FPS).

Lithium and More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

How it was measured: a real Fabric client on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H (an entry-level gaming laptop from 2020) joined the same world at 1280x720, render distance 24, VSync off. Each number is the median second of 20-second scenes over 2 to 4 runs. Runs without mods differed by up to 23%, so smaller changes are not counted. Frame rates depend on the computer: on a faster one the numbers here are usually higher. The percentage is the better guide, but it moves too: mods that cut rendering work (Sodium, More Culling) gain most when the graphics card is the limit, and mods that cut entities or game logic (Entity Culling, Lithium) when the processor is. Some effects belong to one GPU brand: Sodium's drop with mobs on 1.21.1 and 1.20.1 only happens on NVIDIA cards. Raw data: every run's frame rate per second, Minecraft and Fabric Loader versions, GPU and driver, loaded mods and the SHA-1 of each jar, which Modrinth matches to the exact release.

About this data

Loaded a world: a real Minecraft client started with the mod and joined a world. Server started: a real server finished starting with it. Benchmark: it ran through a timed lab scene. Did not start: the game stopped before it was playable.

Each row is one run in the Modkeel lab, matched to the exact file that ran: other files may behave differently. A mod or pair missing here was not tested yet; that is not a conflict.
