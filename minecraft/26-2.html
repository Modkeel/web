Lab data

Mods tested on Minecraft 26.2

Sodium mc26.2-0.9.2-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.11.4+26.2-fabric ran together in a client benchmark on Minecraft 26.2 in the Modkeel lab. Releases of 16 mods, among them Athena, Dynamic Trees and End Remastered, made for an older Minecraft version did not start on 26.2 in the Modkeel lab: use a release made for 26.2. 24 mod releases started in real Minecraft 26.2 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 16 pairs of mods in the same game.

Lab data as of October 4, 2026. 24 releases ran, 16 pairs, 16 older releases did not start, 22 ports.

What the lab measured on 26.2

Frame rate on Minecraft 26.2 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 70% with 300 mobs in view (110 to 188 FPS), 37% over terrain (286 to 391 FPS) and 102% with 300 mobs behind a wall (87 to 176 FPS).

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 61% with 300 mobs in view (110 to 178 FPS), 33% over terrain (286 to 380 FPS) and 97% with 300 mobs behind a wall (87 to 172 FPS).

Lithium, Entity Culling, ImmediatelyFast and More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view or over terrain by more than runs without mods differed.

How it was measured: a real Fabric client on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H joined the same world at 1280x720, render distance 24, VSync off. Each number is the median second of 20-second scenes over 2 to 6 runs. Runs without mods differed by up to 20%, so smaller changes are not counted. Raw data: every run's frame rate per second, Minecraft and Fabric Loader versions, GPU and driver, loaded mods and the SHA-1 of each jar, which Modrinth matches to the exact release.

About this data

Loaded a world: a real Minecraft client started with the mod and joined a world. Server started: a real server finished starting with it. Benchmark: it ran through a timed lab scene. Did not start: the game stopped before it was playable.

Each row is one run in the Modkeel lab, matched to the exact file that ran: other files may behave differently. A mod or pair missing here was not tested yet; that is not a conflict.
