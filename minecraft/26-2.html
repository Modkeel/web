Which mods work on Minecraft 26.2

As of October 14, 2026, 22 of the most downloaded Modrinth mods have a release build for Minecraft 26.2 but not for 26.3 on at least one loader, among them Just Enough Items (JEI), Simple Voice Chat, oωo (owo-lib), Curios API and Cubes Without Borders.

As of October 14, 2026, 199 of the 300 most downloaded Fabric mods (66%) and 183 NeoForge mods (61%) have a release build for Minecraft 26.2; 11 of them started in a real 26.2 game in the Modkeel lab. Sodium mc26.2-0.9.2-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.11.4+26.2-fabric ran together in a client benchmark on Minecraft 26.2 in the Modkeel lab. Releases of 16 mods, among them Athena, Dynamic Trees and End Remastered, made for an older Minecraft version did not start on 26.2 in the Modkeel lab: use a release made for 26.2. 23 mod releases started in real Minecraft 26.2 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 16 pairs of mods in the same game.

Staying on 26.2?

22 of the most downloaded mods have a release build for 26.2 but not yet for Minecraft 26.3 on at least one loader, among them Just Enough Items (JEI), Simple Voice Chat, oωo (owo-lib), Curios API and Cubes Without Borders. Until they update, 26.2 is the version where they all have a release.

What the lab measured on 26.2

Frame rate on Minecraft 26.2 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 61% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 179 FPS), 37% over terrain (283 to 388 FPS) and 88% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 168 FPS).

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 56% with 300 mobs in view (111 to 173 FPS), 32% over terrain (283 to 373 FPS) and 85% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 165 FPS).

Entity Culling raised the frame rate 76% with 300 mobs behind a wall (89 to 157 FPS).

Lithium, ImmediatelyFast and More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 21 runs per number; changes within 35%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.

Server optimizers on 26.2

The lab ran 15 server optimizers on Minecraft 26.2, each alone on a fresh Fabric server, against 8 runs without them (the scenes and the rule).

Server start: Lithium (-6%) beat every run without it.

Memory after start: FerriteCore (-6%) and ModernFix-mVUS (-5%) beat every run without them; Moonrise (+82%), Lithium (+8%), C2ME (+8%) and Async (+4%) lost to every one.

400 mobs: Async (-60%), Lithium (-56%), Moonrise (-27%) and LazyAI (-16%) beat every run without them.

Redstone: Alternate Current (-85%), Lithium (-52%), Moonrise (-33%), ModernFix-mVUS (-12%), Ksyxis (-12%) and Fast Noise (-12%) beat every run without them; FerriteCore (+16%), Async (+15%) and ServerCore (+7%) lost to every one.

New terrain: C2ME (-52%) and Moonrise (-24%) beat every run without them; Async (+8%) and ModernFix-mVUS (+6%) lost to every one.

Memory after new terrain: C2ME (-54%) beat every run without it; ScalableLux (+160%), Moonrise (+60%) and Lithium (+29%) lost to every one.

The five most common together (Lithium, C2ME, FerriteCore, ScalableLux and Very Many Players) beat every run without them on 400 mobs (-48%), redstone (-34%), new terrain (-55%) and memory after new terrain (-49%); they lost to every one on memory after start (+11%).

Krypton, Structure Layout Optimizer and Very Many Players beat or lost to no scene clearly: on a plain server the benchmark cannot tell what they change.
