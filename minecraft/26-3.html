Which mods work on Minecraft 26.3

39 days after 26.3 came out on September 15, 2026, this many of the top 300 Fabric mods had a release build for it: 190, more than 26.2's 186 at the same age. A week after release: 139, fewer than 26.2's 155.

As of October 24, 2026, 190 of the 300 most downloaded Fabric mods (63%) and 172 NeoForge mods (57%) have a release build for Minecraft 26.3; 13 of them started in a real 26.3 game in the Modkeel lab. Sodium mc26.3-0.9.3-alpha.1-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.11.7+26.3-fabric ran together in a client benchmark on Minecraft 26.3 in the Modkeel lab. Releases of 13 mods, among them Bobby, Chat Patches and CustomSkinLoader, made for an older Minecraft version did not start on 26.3 in the Modkeel lab: use a release made for 26.3. 25 mod releases started in real Minecraft 26.3 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 11 pairs of mods in the same game.

What the lab measured on 26.3

Frame rate on Minecraft 26.3 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 55% with 300 mobs in view (94 to 145 FPS), 23% over terrain (234 to 288 FPS) and 78% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 256 FPS).

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 56% with 300 mobs in view (94 to 146 FPS), 13% over terrain (234 to 264 FPS) and 73% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 250 FPS).

Entity Culling raised the frame rate 110% with 300 mobs behind a wall (144 to 303 FPS).

Lithium, ImmediatelyFast and More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

Measured on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H, 2 to 4 runs per number; changes within 15%, the spread of runs without mods, are not counted. How the lab measures · raw data.

Server optimizers on 26.3

The lab ran 15 server optimizers on Minecraft 26.3, each alone on a fresh Fabric server, against 4 runs without them (the scenes and the rule).

Memory after start: FerriteCore (-6%) and ModernFix-mVUS (-5%) beat every run without them; Moonrise (+76%), Lithium (+6%), C2ME (+5%) and Async (+4%) lost to every one.

400 mobs: Async (-62%), Lithium (-52%), Moonrise (-18%) and LazyAI (-9%) beat every run without them.

Redstone: Alternate Current (-81%), Lithium (-37%) and Moonrise (-18%) beat every run without them; Async (+20%) and FerriteCore (+6%) lost to every one.

New terrain: C2ME (-15%), ScalableLux (-10%), Lithium (-5%) and ModernFix-mVUS (-5%) beat every run without them.

Memory after new terrain: C2ME (-56%) and Moonrise (-19%) beat every run without them; ScalableLux (+118%) lost to every one.

The five most common together (Lithium, C2ME, FerriteCore, ScalableLux and Very Many Players) beat every run without them on 400 mobs (-52%), redstone (-27%), new terrain (-24%) and memory after new terrain (-51%); they lost to every one on memory after start (+8%).

Fast Noise, Krypton, Ksyxis, ServerCore, Structure Layout Optimizer and Very Many Players beat or lost to no scene clearly: on a plain server the benchmark cannot tell what they change.
