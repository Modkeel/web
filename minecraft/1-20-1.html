Mods tested on Minecraft 1.20.1

Sodium mc1.20.1-0.5.13-fabric and Iris Shaders 1.7.6+1.20.1 loaded a world together on a client on Minecraft 1.20.1 in the Modkeel lab. 14 mod releases started in real Minecraft 1.20.1 games in the Modkeel lab, among them Sodium, Iris Shaders, Lithium, FerriteCore and Entity Culling. The Modkeel lab also ran 42 pairs of mods in the same game.

Lab data as of October 4, 2026. 14 releases ran, 42 pairs.

What the lab measured on 1.20.1

Frame rate on Minecraft 1.20.1 with each mod, against the same game without mods.

Sodium raised the frame rate 122% over terrain (227 to 504 FPS) and 26% with 300 mobs behind a wall (58 to 74 FPS); it lowered the frame rate 11% with 300 mobs in view (84 to 74 FPS), because on Windows with an NVIDIA card Sodium turns on a driver workaround against crashes that makes each draw call wait, and this version draws mobs of mixed kinds in many small draw calls.

Iris Shaders, which runs on Sodium and had no shader pack loaded, raised the frame rate 106% with 300 mobs in view (84 to 173 FPS), 117% over terrain (227 to 494 FPS) and 166% with 300 mobs behind a wall (58 to 156 FPS).

Lithium, which speeds up game logic rather than drawing, lowered the frame rate 16% over terrain (227 to 192 FPS).

Entity Culling raised the frame rate 124% with 300 mobs behind a wall (58 to 131 FPS).

ImmediatelyFast raised the frame rate 14% with 300 mobs in view (84 to 96 FPS).

More Culling did not change the frame rate with 300 mobs in view, over terrain or with 300 mobs behind a wall by more than runs without mods differed.

How it was measured: a real Fabric client on a laptop with a GeForce GTX 1650 and a Core i7-10750H joined the same world at 1280x720, render distance 24, VSync off. Each number is the median second of 20-second scenes over 2 to 4 runs. Runs without mods differed by up to 21%, so smaller changes are not counted. Raw data: every run's frame rate per second, Minecraft and Fabric Loader versions, GPU and driver, loaded mods and the SHA-1 of each jar, which Modrinth matches to the exact release.

About this data

Loaded a world: a real Minecraft client started with the mod and joined a world. Server started: a real server finished starting with it. Benchmark: it ran through a timed lab scene. Did not start: the game stopped before it was playable.

Each row is one run in the Modkeel lab, matched to the exact file that ran: other files may behave differently. A mod or pair missing here was not tested yet; that is not a conflict.
